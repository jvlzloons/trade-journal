# 📓💹 Trade Journal for Forex  

Welcome to my **Trade Journal** — a simple but powerful tool I built to track and analyze my Forex trades.  
This project helps me (and maybe you too!) stay consistent, accountable, and data-driven in trading.  

---

## ✨ Features  

- 📝 **Add & Manage Trades** – Log your entries, exits, risk, and outcomes.  
- 📊 **Performance Tracking** – Keep an eye on win rate, profit factor, and expectancy.  
- 📷 **Image Uploads** – Paste screenshots or charts of your setups directly into the journal.  
- 💼 **Prop Firm Tracker** – Track challenge purchases, payouts, and breaches. Each purchase is paid from your payout balance first and the rest from your own money, so you see what you spent from profits vs. out of pocket and your net profit.
- ✅ **Trading Checklist** – Ensure you follow your process before entering trades (W1 → H4 → m15, etc.).  
- ⚡ **Fast & Responsive** – Built with a lightweight backend and clean frontend for smooth usage.
